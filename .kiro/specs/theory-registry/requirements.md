# Requirements Document: Theory Registry

## Introduction

The Theory Registry is a module that tracks, evaluates, prioritizes, and manages known investigative theories across all tenants. Unlike the Taxonomy Engine (which discovers new patterns in raw data), the Theory Registry manages KNOWN theories — claims that have already been articulated by researchers, documentarians, or communities — and evaluates their proof status against formal standards. The module tells researchers exactly which theories are closest to being proven, which are disproven, and what specific evidence gaps must be filled to change a theory's status. The initial seed comes from Ancient Aliens episode claims ("ancient astronaut theorists suggest...") expanded via web search to include theories from independent researchers (Hancock, Bauval, Carlson, West, Schoch, etc.) and academic literature.

## Glossary

- **Theory**: A specific, testable claim about the world (e.g., "The Giza pyramids functioned as power plants"). A theory is NOT a topic or category — it is a falsifiable proposition.
- **Theory_Registry**: The persistent store of all known theories with their current proof status, evidence inventory, priority score, and research directions.
- **Proof_Status**: The current evaluation state of a theory: PROVEN, UNPROVEN, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, or DISPROVEN. Generated by the Proof Engine.
- **Evidence_Inventory**: The collection of evidence items (for and against) associated with a theory, each scored for quality and relevance.
- **Testability_Score**: A metric (0.0 to 1.0) indicating how feasible it is to obtain the evidence needed to prove or disprove the theory with current technology and access.
- **Priority_Score**: A composite ranking that determines which theories should be investigated first, based on: closeness to proof threshold, testability, theory cluster connections, and community interest.
- **Theory_Cluster**: A group of theories where proving one shifts the probability of related ones (e.g., "pyramids are power plants" + "ancient civilizations had electricity" + "Baghdad Battery was functional").
- **Evidence_Gap**: A specific, named piece of evidence that, if obtained, would change the theory's proof status.
- **Research_Direction**: An actionable instruction telling the researcher exactly what to do to fill an evidence gap.
- **Source_Theory**: The origin of the theory (Ancient Aliens episode, researcher publication, community submission, etc.)
- **Cascade_Effect**: When proving Theory A automatically increases the proof score of Theory B because they share evidence or are logically connected.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Theory Intake and Registration

**User Story:** As a research analyst, I want to register known theories from multiple sources (Ancient Aliens, academic literature, researcher publications), so that the system tracks all testable claims in a structured format.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Theory_Registry SHALL accept theory submissions containing: title (max 200 chars), description (max 2000 chars), source (e.g., "Ancient Aliens S04E07", "Bauval 1994", "community submission"), category (ancient_mysteries, conspiracy, crime, etc.), and initial_evidence (array of evidence items)
2. THE Theory_Registry SHALL validate that each submitted theory is falsifiable by checking that the description contains at least one testable prediction (verified via Bedrock Claude analysis)
3. THE Theory_Registry SHALL assign each registered theory a unique theory_id (UUID) and store it in Aurora with created_at timestamp and source_theory attribution
4. WHEN a theory is registered, THE Theory_Registry SHALL automatically invoke the Proof Engine to generate an initial proof evaluation against the tenant's default standard
5. THE Theory_Registry SHALL support bulk import of theories from structured sources (JSON array of theory objects) with a minimum throughput of 100 theories per minute
6. THE Theory_Registry SHALL detect and flag duplicate or near-duplicate theories (cosine similarity > 0.90 between theory description embeddings) and present them for merge review rather than creating duplicates
7. WHEN seeding from Ancient Aliens content, THE Theory_Registry SHALL extract claims prefixed by or containing "ancient astronaut theorists suggest", "could it be that", "is it possible that", or similar speculative framing language, and reformulate each as a specific testable proposition

### Requirement 2: Evidence Inventory Management

**User Story:** As a research analyst, I want to attach evidence items (for and against) to each theory with quality ratings, so that the system can calculate accurate proof scores.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Theory_Registry SHALL support attaching Evidence_Items to any registered theory, where each item contains: description, type (documentary, physical, testimonial, statistical, experimental), stance (supports, contradicts, neutral), quality_score (0.0 to 1.0), source_citation, and verified_by (who confirmed this evidence exists)
2. WHEN an Evidence_Item is attached to a theory, THE Theory_Registry SHALL automatically re-run the Proof Engine evaluation for that theory and update the Proof_Status
3. THE Theory_Registry SHALL calculate the Evidence_Inventory completeness as the ratio of filled evidence gaps to total identified evidence gaps (0.0 to 1.0)
4. THE Theory_Registry SHALL distinguish between evidence that the system found automatically (via taxonomy scanning) and evidence submitted by researchers manually
5. WHEN the same Evidence_Item is relevant to multiple theories, THE Theory_Registry SHALL link it to all relevant theories and trigger Cascade_Effect re-evaluation on all linked theories

### Requirement 3: Priority Scoring and Ranking

**User Story:** As a research analyst, I want theories ranked by investigation priority, so that I focus effort on the theories most likely to yield results.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Theory_Registry SHALL calculate a Priority_Score (0.0 to 1.0) for each theory using the formula: `priority = (closeness_to_threshold × 0.35) + (testability_score × 0.25) + (cluster_bonus × 0.20) + (community_interest × 0.20)`
2. THE Theory_Registry SHALL calculate closeness_to_threshold as: `1.0 - abs(current_score - proof_threshold) / proof_threshold` — theories closer to the proof threshold rank higher
3. THE Theory_Registry SHALL calculate Testability_Score based on: whether required evidence is physically obtainable, whether the test is affordable, and whether the technology exists to perform it
4. THE Theory_Registry SHALL calculate cluster_bonus as: `connected_theories_count / max_connected_theories` — theories in larger clusters rank higher because proving them has broader impact
5. THE Theory_Registry SHALL expose a GET endpoint at `/theories/priority-queue` returning theories sorted by Priority_Score descending, with optional filters for category, status, and minimum_testability
6. WHEN a theory's Priority_Score changes (due to new evidence, cluster connection, or re-evaluation), THE Theory_Registry SHALL re-rank the priority queue within 60 seconds

### Requirement 4: Theory Clusters and Cascade Effects

**User Story:** As a research analyst, I want the system to identify theories that are logically connected (where proving one shifts probability of others), so that I can maximize investigation impact by targeting cluster hubs.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Theory_Registry SHALL automatically detect Theory_Clusters by identifying theories that share: evidence items (same evidence supports multiple theories), logical dependencies (if A is true, B becomes more likely), or common entities/locations
2. WHEN a theory's Proof_Status changes, THE Theory_Registry SHALL propagate a Cascade_Effect to all theories in its cluster, re-calculating their scores with a cascade_weight proportional to the logical connection strength (0.1 to 0.5)
3. THE Theory_Registry SHALL store cluster relationships in Neptune as `logically_connects` edges between Theory vertices, with properties: connection_type (shared_evidence, logical_dependency, geographic_proximity), connection_strength (0.0 to 1.0), and justification_text
4. THE Theory_Registry SHALL expose a GET endpoint at `/theories/clusters` returning all identified clusters with their member theories, hub theory (most connected), and aggregate cluster status
5. WHEN a theory is proven (PROVEN status), THE Theory_Registry SHALL highlight its cluster members as "high priority" investigations because a related theory has been confirmed

### Requirement 5: Research Direction Generation

**User Story:** As a research analyst, I want specific, actionable research directions for each theory, so that I know exactly what investigation to perform next.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a theory has Proof_Status of INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, THE Theory_Registry SHALL generate a list of Research_Directions: specific, actionable steps that would fill the identified Evidence_Gaps
2. EACH Research_Direction SHALL contain: description (what to do), expected_outcome (what finding would change the status), difficulty (easy/medium/hard), estimated_cost (free/low/medium/high), and testability (can this actually be done with current technology?)
3. THE Theory_Registry SHALL rank Research_Directions by impact: directions that would satisfy Critical_Items in the proof checklist rank higher than those satisfying non-critical items
4. WHEN a theory has Proof_Status of DISPROVEN, THE Theory_Registry SHALL generate a "rehabilitation path" — the specific evidence that would overturn the disproof, clearly labeled as requiring extraordinary evidence
5. THE Theory_Registry SHALL update Research_Directions automatically when new evidence is attached or when the Proof Engine re-evaluates the theory

### Requirement 6: Ancient Aliens Seed Integration

**User Story:** As a platform operator, I want to seed the Theory Registry from Ancient Aliens episode content and then expand via web search to related researchers, so that we start with a comprehensive corpus of testable theories.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Theory_Registry SHALL support importing theories extracted from Ancient Aliens episode descriptions, transcripts, or summaries, reformulating speculative claims into testable propositions
2. WHEN Ancient Aliens theories are imported, THE Theory_Registry SHALL use Bedrock Claude to identify the core testable claim, strip rhetorical framing, and produce a neutral falsifiable statement (e.g., "Could the pyramids be power plants?" → "The internal structure of the Giza pyramids generated or conducted electrical energy")
3. AFTER Ancient Aliens seed is complete, THE Theory_Registry SHALL initiate a web search expansion phase using Tavily to find additional theories from: Graham Hancock, Robert Bauval, Randall Carlson, Robert Schoch, John Anthony West, Christopher Dunn, Brien Foerster, and academic archaeoastronomy literature
4. THE Theory_Registry SHALL tag each theory with its source_type: "ancient_aliens", "researcher_publication", "academic_paper", "community_submission", or "web_discovery"
5. THE Theory_Registry SHALL deduplicate theories found across multiple sources (Ancient Aliens + researcher + web) by merging them into a single theory entry with multiple source attributions
6. WHEN the seed and expansion phases are complete, THE Theory_Registry SHALL produce a seed report showing: total theories registered, theories per source type, initial proof status distribution, and top 10 by priority score

### Requirement 7: User-Driven Evidence Submission

**User Story:** As a community researcher, I want to submit evidence that changes a theory's proof status, so that the system evolves as new information becomes available.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Theory_Registry SHALL expose a POST endpoint at `/theories/{theory_id}/evidence` accepting new Evidence_Items with: description, type, stance, source_citation, and optional file_attachment (S3 reference)
2. WHEN evidence is submitted, THE Theory_Registry SHALL queue it for quality assessment by the Proof Engine before updating the theory's proof evaluation
3. THE Theory_Registry SHALL track evidence provenance: who submitted it, when, and whether it has been independently verified
4. WHEN user-submitted evidence changes a theory from INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE to PROVEN or from INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE to DISPROVEN, THE Theory_Registry SHALL generate an alert and log the event to the audit trail
5. THE Theory_Registry SHALL expose a GET endpoint at `/theories/{theory_id}/history` returning the full chronological history of all evidence submissions and proof status changes for that theory
