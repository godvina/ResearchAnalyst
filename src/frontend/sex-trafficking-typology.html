SEX TRAFFICKING CRIME TYPOLOGY

🔴 Pattern Recognition Lens

🎭 Recruitment & Grooming
83% social media
Social media contact patterns, age disparity, gift-giving, isolation from family, false employment promises, Romeo pimp tactics
EVIDENCE EXAMPLE
Subject A (age 42) contacted 6 females aged 16-19 via Instagram over 3 months. Initial flattery within 24hrs, gifts by day 3, requests to relocate by week 2. 4 of 6 targets isolated from family within 30 days. Typology flags: love bombing + age disparity + communication isolation + geographic relocation.
83% of sex trafficking victims were recruited by someone they knew or met through social media. Average recruiter-victim age gap: 14 years. (DOJ National Human Trafficking Hotline Data, 2023)
✈️ Transportation & Movement
71% interstate
Hotel patterns and short-stay bookings, interstate travel, charter/private aviation, multiple cities in compressed timeframes, circuit rotation schedules
EVIDENCE EXAMPLE
Subject B booked hotels in 4 cities over 9 days (Miami→Atlanta→Charlotte→DC). All under alias, paid by Subject A's prepaid card. Victim C's phone GPS confirms presence at each location. Pattern repeats on 14-day cycle. Typology flags: hotel clustering + geographic velocity + circuit rotation + third-party booking.
71% of federally prosecuted sex trafficking cases involved interstate transportation. Average circuit covers 4.2 cities over 2-week rotation. (USSC Sourcebook 2019-2023, 18 U.S.C. § 1591 cases)
📱 Communication Networks
94% multi-phone
Burner phone clusters, encrypted app usage, coded language in ads/messages, scheduling coordination patterns, star topology networks
EVIDENCE EXAMPLE
8 prepaid phones activated same day, same Walmart location. Star topology with Subject A's primary phone as central hub connecting to all 8. Phones used exclusively for ad posting on 3 platforms. Activity spikes 4-6pm daily (booking coordination window). Typology flags: disposable cluster + star topology + coordination window + platform spread.
94% of sex trafficking operations use multiple phones/accounts. Average operation manages 6.3 advertising accounts across 2.8 platforms. (Thorn/Spotlight Program Data, 2023)
🏦 Financial Control
62% structuring
Structuring deposits below CTR threshold, victim accounts controlled by others, prepaid card networks, shell LLCs, cryptocurrency for ad payments, quota ledgers
EVIDENCE EXAMPLE
12 bank accounts opened in victims' names at 4 different banks. All accounts show identical deposit patterns: $8,500-$9,800 cash deposits 2-3x/week. Subject A is authorized signer on all 12. Daily ledger on phone shows $1,000/night quota per victim. Typology flags: structuring + controlled accounts + quota evidence + shell entity.
Structuring/Money Laundering present in 62% of sex trafficking prosecutions. Average trafficker controls 3.7 victim bank accounts. (FinCEN SAR Trend Analysis, FY2022-2024)
🏨 Venue & Infrastructure
55% residential
Massage parlor/spa fronts, hotel room rotation, residential brothels in suburbs, online platform ad cadence, multi-location operations under same control
EVIDENCE EXAMPLE
Subject A leases 3 apartments in different zip codes under LLC "Serenity Wellness." Each occupied 2-3 days/week on rotation. Online ads match rotation schedule exactly — new city posted as previous location goes dark. Neighbors report high foot traffic of males at odd hours. Typology flags: venue rotation + ad posting cadence + multi-location operation + front business.
55% of sex trafficking venues are residential properties. Average operation rotates across 3.1 locations on 2-4 day cycles. Hotel-based operations average 4.7 different rooms/week. (Polaris Project Typology Study, 2024)
⛓️ Power & Control
78% debt bondage
Debt bondage records/ledgers, document confiscation (IDs, passports), threats to victim or family, rules/quota enforcement, branding/tattoos, GPS surveillance
EVIDENCE EXAMPLE
Ledger recovered from Subject A's phone showing 4 victims with running "debt" balances ($15K-$45K each). Charges include "rent" $3K/mo, "protection" $500/wk, "clothes" $2K. Daily quota: $1,000/night minimum. 3 victims' passports found in Subject A's safe deposit box. Typology flags: debt ledger + document control + quota enforcement + surveillance control.
78% of sex trafficking victims report debt bondage. 63% had identity documents confiscated. Average daily quota: $500-$1,000. Avg sentence: 168 months. (DOJ Trafficking in Persons Report, 2024; USSC)
