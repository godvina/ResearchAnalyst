📐 Scoring Algorithm Explanation ▶

Backlog Score is a weighted composite of 7 factors (0–100 scale):

FactorWeightNormalization
Pre-Assessment Score25%Direct (0–100)
Priority Level20%critical=100, high=75, medium=50, low=25
Classification Confidence15%Direct percentage (0–100)
Estimated Harm15%Log scale: $0=0, $1M≈50, $100M≈75, $1B+=100
Policy Alignment10%0=none, 50=low boost, 75=med, 100=high
OSINT Sources10%Linear: 0→0, 7+→100
Urgency (Age)5%min(100, days×2) — caps at 50 days

Worked Example: A lead with priority=high (75), confidence=85%, pre-assessment=70, 4 OSINT sources (57), $50M harm (68), policy match at 1.7x (100), 20 days old (40):

Score = 70×0.25 + 75×0.20 + 85×0.15 + 68×0.15 + 100×0.10 + 57×0.10 + 40×0.05 = 72.5
